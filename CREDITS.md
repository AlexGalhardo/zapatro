## Art and audio

- Cards, card backs, modifiers, Manha symbols, panels and backgrounds are vector art drawn by code (`src/art/flat.ts`).
- Character portraits (128×128 pixel art) are converted locally from concept art made with ChatGPT image generation (`scripts/portraits.ts`); O Mudo's portrait was generated with [PixelLab](https://www.pixellab.ai). The UI icon sheet is ChatGPT concept art cut by `scripts/icons.ts`.
- Store, press and Open Graph art is composed by `scripts/store-art.ts` from the game's own cards and portraits.
- Music, sound effects and voices generated with [ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io).
- Commercial use depends on paid plans on both platforms (to be confirmed before release, PLAN RC4.5).

## Inspiration

Zapatro is an original game built on the traditional Brazilian card game **Truco Paulista**, with a run structure inspired by **Balatro** (LocalThunk / Playstack). No art, code, names or layouts from other games are used.
